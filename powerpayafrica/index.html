Pioneering Energy Visibility Beyond the Meter using AI

PowerPay AI technology provides granular insights into household or SME energy consumption trends by breaking down overall energy usage into individual appliance components through the application of advanced Machine Learning techniques. With the help of this technology, users can identify and keep an eye on the performance of individual appliances without requiring separate meters. The data enables users to make well-informed decisions to minimize energy waste and increase efficiency while maximizing appliance utilization. Furthermore, our technology works flawlessly with smart home systems, providing cloud services for real-time monitoring and control. By lowering overall energy use, this invention not only enables consumers to save on energy expenses but also contributes to larger sustainability initiatives.
